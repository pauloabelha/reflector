# Draft validation v3: construction and equilibration

Status: design draft, not preregistered and not yet executed.

Validation v2 showed that context-sharing schema families and learned
procedures can improve efficiency on known synthetic families. V3 must
distinguish useful control compression from higher-order construction. Its
design follows the authoritative source survey in
`references/AUTHORITATIVE_REFLECTING_ABSTRACTION_SURVEY.md`.

## Claim boundary

V3 may test whether a Kaggle-exportable symbolic agent:

- accommodates a perturbation by reorganizing a learned structure;
- distinguishes temporal procedures from relations among transformations;
- composes and reverses learned operations;
- makes calibrated modal reachability predictions;
- and treats its own learned operative structures as inputs to a subsequent
  construction.

It cannot establish psychological fidelity, consciousness, Piagetian stages,
or ARC-AGI-3 generalization.

## Proposed interactive families

### 1. Perturbation accommodation

A stable action/effect relation holds in several contexts. A new factor creates
an apparent contradiction. Successful agents must differentiate the context,
retain the old relation in its valid domain, and integrate both cases into a
single conditional structure.

Required ablations:

- confidence decay without structural reorganization;
- differentiation without reintegration;
- no negative evidence.

### 2. Composition transfer

Two transformations are learned independently. A novel layout can be solved
only by composing their effects. The composed transformation must not have
appeared as a memorized action sequence.

Required ablations:

- procedures only;
- no coordination operator;
- no abstraction.

### 3. Reversal transfer

The agent learns a transformation in one direction, then must reach a goal by
constructing or identifying its inverse in a novel context.

Required ablations:

- forward schemas only;
- no reversal operator;
- brute-force exploration under the same budget.

### 4. Modal reachability

The same visual inventory admits goals that are possible, impossible, or
necessary under different operator sets. The agent is rewarded for avoiding
provably fruitless actions and for selecting a plan whose predicted
reachability is borne out by interaction.

Modal claims must be calibrated against exhaustive state graphs generated by
the diagnostic environment, never accepted from the agent's self-description.

### 5. Knowing-level intervention

Several learned procedures solve training layouts. A held-out task requires
comparison or transformation of those learned procedures themselves—for
example, selecting their common operation, composing two procedure effects, or
reversing one—rather than replaying any procedure.

The crucial ablation preserves every controller and procedure but removes
access to their serialized structure. A positive effect against that ablation
is the minimum evidence that the mechanism is more than a control hierarchy.

### 6. Morphism composition

The agent observes several transformations over different contents and must
learn typed comparisons between their source/result structures. A held-out task
requires composing two compatible comparisons; neither the resulting mapping
nor a corresponding action sequence appears during training.

The accepted finite comparison structure must expose:

- transformation objects;
- mappings with explicit domains and codomains;
- rejection of mismatched compositions;
- identity mappings;
- closed composition for the tested cases;
- identity and associativity checks.

Required ablations:

- similarity-based schema families without mappings;
- isolated mappings without composition;
- temporal procedure composition without comparison objects;
- untyped composition.

Success means a small executable comparison algebra improved held-out
prediction or control. It does not justify claiming that general category
theory explains cognition.

## Evidence records required before execution

Each proposed higher-order construction must serialize:

- source structures and action/effect evidence;
- construction operator;
- projected representation;
- reorganized representation;
- typed source and target for comparison structures;
- predicted new capability;
- positive and negative applicability evidence;
- contradictions encountered;
- accommodation or integration performed;
- retirement/specialization history;
- measured causal effect under ablation.

## Freeze requirements

Before viewing a confirmation split:

1. implement all environments and exhaustive oracles;
2. fix action budgets, seeds, thresholds, bootstrap method, and baselines;
3. demonstrate that each task is solvable by an independent minimal oracle;
4. demonstrate that each designated ablation fails for the intended reason;
5. preserve official `bt11`, export, network-disabled smoke, and resource gates;
6. publish development failures and revisions;
7. hash the protocol and code state.

Only then may an untouched confirmation split be executed once.
