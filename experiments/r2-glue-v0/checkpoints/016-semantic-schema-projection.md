# Checkpoint 016: semantic schema projection

Time: 2026-08-12

## Failure

Qwen could maintain a natural-language explanation, propose one measurable
top-level goal, or compose existing stable schema IDs. It could not deposit a
novel relational/dynamic abduction into the recursive epistemic engine. A
concept such as coupling could remain prose while R2 independently pursued an
alignment or contact residual, with no typed record joining the explanation to
entities, actions, predictions, counterconditions, and settlement.

## Generic contract

Ordinary semantic writes now contain a bounded `schema_hypotheses` array. Each
hypothesis declares:

- a relational, dynamic, or relational-dynamic kind;
- canonical `actor,target` spatial ports;
- one generic relation family and action-free claim;
- a reference to one independently measurable `goal_proposal`;
- one to four predicted structural dynamics;
- one to four explicit counterconditions; and
- calibrated model confidence plus its evidence basis.

The prompt never names magnets, AR25, colors, coordinates, actions, or a game
solution. It tells the model that analogical labels are shorthand for
falsifiable structure, not ontology or fact.

## Projection and authority

- The compiler validates the closed types and goal reference, deduplicates the
  hypothesis, resets empirical support to zero, and converts confidence to a
  three-level attention prior.
- R2 creates an ordinary recursive `semantic-schema-hypothesis` over the
  situated role and potential bindings using `semantic-proposal` authority.
- Every exact legal command receives a projection containing grounded entity
  roles, supported or open actor/target effects, residual prediction, declared
  dynamic predictions, and explicit evidence counters.
- Confidence is a final attention/probe tie-break only. It cannot satisfy
  identity, mechanism, progress, plan, or environment-terminal gates.
- Environment settlement measures generic relation dynamics such as relative
  position, coherent motion, contact state, containment state, component
  count, and intrinsic-geometry preservation.
- A declared dynamic supports the schema only when observed by a diagnostic
  command (or when a previously open effect reveals the declared dynamic).
  Failure of a command that did not instantiate the dynamic remains OPEN.
- Schema support/refutation, goal-frontier progress, mechanism evidence, CAE
  support, and environment terminals remain independent.

## Evidence

- A high-confidence fixture compiles with `attention_priority=3`,
  `empirical_support=0`, and no control authority.
- Its recursive fact has `semantic-proposal` rather than environment authority.
- A novel relative-position change can support the explanatory schema while
  the action mechanism is only `OBSERVED`.
- A goal-nonprogress countercondition can refute the explanatory schema even
  when one declared structural dynamic occurred; that dynamic does not borrow
  support across the countercondition.
- Five focused semantic projection tests plus the surrounding runtime,
  planner, CAE, and Arcade suite pass; the focused aggregate is 155 tests.
- The full suite has 260 passes and the one unchanged missing historical
  `parallel-cognitive-workspace-v1-16/artifacts/SUMMARY.json` failure.

## Live evidence

Fresh local-Qwen AR25, with no relation-specific prompt example, emitted one
low-confidence dynamic alignment hypothesis linked to its first measurable
goal. The compiler stored zero support and attention priority one. R2 created a
recursive schema/binding pair, grounded actor and target, and projected the
hypothesis onto the exact command as `grounded-open-action-effect`.

On the first environment settlement, neither declared contact nor containment
change occurred and neither had been predicted for that command. R2 recorded
both as `OPEN: command-not-diagnostic-for-dynamic`, left support and refutation
at zero, and retained the environment evidence reference. It neither rewarded
the prose nor punished an uninformative probe. A separate fresh trace then
showed the linked goal residual advancing 65 to 62 while the movement mechanism
was confirmed, demonstrating that those axes can change without granting the
schema support.

Scene-wide coherent motion is treated as a reference-frame confound rather
than evidence for coupling; it remains OPEN unless a non-global diagnostic
transition isolates the relation.

## Limit

The live model's hypothesis was weak and no diagnostic command has yet
supported or refuted it. There is no level, score, sample-efficiency, or
cross-game improvement claim. Checkpoint 017 must test whether different games
produce distinct schema hypotheses, whether R2 reaches diagnostic
interventions, and whether revisions respond to schema-specific evidence
without any prompt or code changes.
