# Checkpoint 006: goal-independent atomic effects

Time: 2026-08-12

## Failure

R2 learned atomic action effects only inside settlement of roles already chosen
by a semantic goal. When Qwen abstained, returned an invalid goal, or had not
yet identified the useful relation, exploration transitions could induce a CAE
but could not populate the ordinary atomic effect model.

## Repair

Unassigned atomic entities may now contribute an action-conditioned
translation or invariance under mutual unique correspondence. Both directed
fits must be UNIQUE, select each other, and exactly preserve shape, area,
outline, and value. Goal-tracked roles are excluded to prevent double counting
and remain under their stricter identity settlement.

When a later goal binds to a prelearned mechanism, open role identity is a
legitimate discriminating probe reason. It is not progress authority. Only an
environment-settled UNIQUE correspondence opens the existing progress/planner
gate.

## Evidence

- Unique synthetic translation is learned without a semantic goal.
- Ambiguous identical entities produce no learned effect.
- A uniquely identifiable deformation is not encoded as rigid translation.
- A later synthetic goal sees the learned mechanism, performs an identity-only
  probe, settles UNIQUE roles, and then becomes progress/plan eligible.
- On predeclared CN04, rigid atomic effects were learned before any active
  semantic explanation; CAE simultaneously retained a coherent three-member
  candidate.
- 132 focused tests pass; the full suite has 244 passes and one unchanged
  missing historical artifact.

No CN04 completion or score gain is claimed.
