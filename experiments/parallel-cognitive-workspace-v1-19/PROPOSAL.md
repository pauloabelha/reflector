# Parallel Cognitive Workspace v1.19

## Question

Can the frozen v1.18 shared-workspace calibration mechanism complete its
prospective causal chain when the exact mandatory epistemic frontier fits?

## Why a new version

The fresh v1.18 shared arm stopped before calibration with a typed
`FrontierBudgetError`: an exact, dependency-closed 9,843-unit ambiguity
frontier could not fit its frozen 6,400-unit allowance. That run is preserved
as `INVALID`; its completed control is not reused.

## Only treatment change

The `generic_prospective` frontier allowance is 10,000 units. A read-only
reconstruction of the v1.18 failure state shows that 10,000 admits the full
28-object mandatory unit—nine competing bindings, twelve entities, two
relation sets, two frames, the schema, its Qwen derivation, and R2 criticism—
without the optional objects that begin entering above this boundary.

Everything else is inherited unchanged from v1.18: fresh wa30 development
pair, empty workspaces, 64 actions, live Qwen schedule and model, DSL/compiler,
calibration controller, typed probe budgets, evidence authority, replay,
counterfactuals, and binary gate. No v1.18 semantic object or trajectory is
injected into either arm.

## Verdict

The inherited v1.18 verdict applies. Capacity/integrity/context/transport
failures are `INVALID`. A valid run lacking the complete calibration-to-control
chain is `FAIL`; a full attributed chain is `MECHANISM_PASS`; level advantage
additionally earns `SCORE_PASS`.

This R2-only/shared pair tests the complete mechanism in the shared arm. It
does not isolate calibration's incremental effect from Qwen; that requires a
later shared-no-calibration/shared-calibration comparison.
